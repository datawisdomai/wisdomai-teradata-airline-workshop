- What business subjects can this airline domain answer, and what are their native grains?

## Profitability
- Review September 2026 route profitability against budget. Show revenue, cost, operating income and operating margin, and identify the largest income gaps.
- Compare passenger-count load factor with distance-weighted load factor for Q3 2026. State the formulas and explain why they differ.

## Operations
- Review September 2026 reliability by operating carrier. Show scheduled flight counts, cancellations, diversions and eligible arrival OTP.
- Which route and departure-hour combinations have the highest recorded departure delays? Include flight counts and avoid causal claims.

## Commercial
- Compare September 2026 flown passenger revenue by channel group and cabin. State eligibility filters and travel-date basis.
- Review ancillary net revenue and contribution by category on a purchase-date basis.

## Loyalty/co-brand
- Review September 2026 loyalty activity by historical month-end tier. Separate miles flows from the closing balance.
- Compare partner remuneration and program cost by card product. Explain why cardholder spend is a different measure.

## Customer experience
- Review NPS by cabin with valid response counts. Explain the promoter, passive and detractor rules.
- Compare service-case volumes and cash compensation by case type, preserving opened-date basis.

## Storage/RAG
- Using only the synthetic workshop notes, summarize baggage-related feedback and cite note IDs. State that the notes are invented teaching data.

## Validation
