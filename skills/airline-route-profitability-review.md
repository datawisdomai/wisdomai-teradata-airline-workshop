---
name: Airline route profitability review
description: Use for route profitability, network economics, PRASM/CASM, and budget performance reviews.
---

1. Resolve period and comparison.
2. Use route-month P&L and verify coverage.
3. Report revenue, operating cost, income and ratio-of-sums margin, load factors, RASM and CASM as relevant.
4. Compare actuals with budget and prior period using matched coverage.
5. Rank material gaps; distinguish size from rate.
6. Explain source units and limitations; do not claim demand forecasts or causal drivers.
