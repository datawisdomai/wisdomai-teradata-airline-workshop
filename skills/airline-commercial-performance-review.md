---
name: Airline commercial performance review
description: Use for ticket sales, channels, cabins, fare products, ancillaries and commercial mix analysis.
---

1. Resolve booking versus travel versus purchase period.
2. Use ticket segment for ticket measures and ancillary sales for net ancillary transactions.
3. State boarded/award/revenue eligibility rules rather than guessing them.
4. Use independent aggregates when comparing facts.
5. Compare revenue, volume, mix and distribution cost.
6. Do not invent named-account commitments or confuse total fare with recognized passenger revenue.
