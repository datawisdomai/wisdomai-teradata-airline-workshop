---
name: Airline operational reliability review
description: Use for punctuality, cancellations, delays, carrier, fleet, or airport reliability reviews.
---

1. Resolve travel period.
2. Use flight-grain view.
3. Count scheduled flights, cancellations and diversions separately.
4. Calculate arrival OTP only among non-cancelled, non-diverted flights.
5. Segment by route, carrier, airport or fleet with volumes.
6. Treat weather and inbound delay as associations; validate joins at airport-day grain.
7. Summarize observations and investigation priorities.
