---
name: Airline loyalty and co-brand review
description: Use for loyalty activity, miles balances, card accounts or partner remuneration analysis.
---

1. Resolve month and historical tier basis.
2. Treat balances, YTD measures and active-account counts as snapshots.
3. Separate customer-month, account-month and product-month facts.
4. Distinguish cardholder spend from airline revenue.
5. Calculate remuneration less program cost for net remuneration.
6. Report activity and balances separately; do not assert retention effects.
